A gaunt crooked humanoid creature crawls continuously straight forward on hands and knees. Its upper back is arched high and rounded like a hunch, head and long neck stretched low ahead. It moves the left hand and right knee forward together, plants them, then advances the right hand and left knee, repeating complete alternating cycles without stopping. Thin bent arms support the narrow torso, long hands spread on the floor. The shoulders are uneven and the head stays tilted as its spine rolls with each deliberate transfer of weight. An unsettling grounded low predatory crawl with continuous forward travel.

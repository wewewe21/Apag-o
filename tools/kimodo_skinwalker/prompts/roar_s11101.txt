A gaunt crooked humanoid makes a single unnatural threatening scream. Its narrow feet stay planted with bent knees, shoulders uneven and spine sharply hunched. It draws its thin elbows back and lifts its long neck and tilted head abruptly, chest unfolding briefly as the claw hands spread tensely beside the torso. The upper body shudders and twists asymmetrically while the jaw opens as though screaming. It then folds its spine and drops the head forward into the original low hunch, arms dangling again. A disturbing tense display without walking.

A thin long limbed crooked humanoid attacks straight ahead once. From a sharply hunched uneven stance it coils by twisting its rounded torso left and drawing one claw hand back near the shoulder. It suddenly takes one lunging forward step, snaps its head and long neck ahead and whips that thin arm forward and downward in a vicious raking strike, the other claw reaching to grab. Its bent knees catch the weight, then the arms recoil and it settles back into its crooked hunch. Clear anticipation, explosive long reaching strike and recovery.
